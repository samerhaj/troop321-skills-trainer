Troop 321 Skills Trainer

Welcome to the island, castaways. Your patrol is a tribe, and every station is a challenge: win it with Scout skills, teamwork and a cool head. Click a station on the map to learn it. Each station has three parts: Learn It (know your skills), Build It (watch the steps), Test Yourself (a quiz). You pick your patrol level once you are inside a station.
